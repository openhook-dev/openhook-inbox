What is a webhook inbox?

    
A webhook inbox is a public URL that receives HTTP requests and stores their bodies and headers for inspection. Openhook gives your AI agent access through MCP, alongside a browser inspector and REST API.

    
Does Openhook replace a tunnel?

    
For inspecting or forwarding stored callbacks, you can use Openhook without opening an inbound port on your laptop. It acknowledges the sender before forwarding. Use a tunnel when the sender needs your local handler’s immediate response.
