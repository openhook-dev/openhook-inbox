Test locally. Receive from anywhere.

Your agent is building a webhook integration on your laptop. GitHub or Stripe needs a public URL to send a test event to.

Openhook gives you that URL, keeps the payload, and lets your agent inspect it or forward it to your local app. No open port on your laptop.

See the local testing workflow

Add Openhook to your agent.

claude mcp add --transport http openhook {{origin}}/mcp

A small loop.

  1. Create an inbox.
  2. Give its URL to the service sending the event.
  3. Your agent reads what arrived and continues.
