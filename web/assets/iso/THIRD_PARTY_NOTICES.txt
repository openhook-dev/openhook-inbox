# Notices

isokit stands on two MIT-licensed projects. Ideas and techniques from both are
adapted here; their notices are reproduced below.

- **Hairline** by Lucas Marques — <https://github.com/lucasmarkes/hairline>.
  The rounded-solid silhouette (the hull of a rounded top and foot, so no
  vertical corner is drawn), the "one live colour" rule, the two motion clocks
  (a 700 ms `(.32, .72, 0, 1)` ease for discrete changes) and loops that sleep
  offscreen.

  > MIT License · Copyright (c) 2026 Lucas Marques

- **iso-figure** (ai-iso-skill) by Tolga Cohce — <https://github.com/MrBongoC/ai-iso-skill>.
  Placing flat drawings on a face with a single SVG `matrix()`, the numbered
  plate with four captions, and "pressing a part must produce real output".

  > MIT License · Copyright (c) 2026 Tolga Cohce
